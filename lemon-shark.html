LEMON SHARK

Negaprion brevirostris

Species Profile

Scientific Classification

Scientific name: Negaprion brevirostris

Family: Carcharhinidae

Common name: Lemon Shark

Physical Characteristics

Lemon sharks are recognisable by their yellowish-brown colouring and two similarly sized dorsal fins. In the sunlight you can see why they earned their name of a lemon!

They can grow to around 3.5 metres long, although size varies.

Habitat and Distribution

Lemon sharks live in warm coastal waters of the western Atlantic Ocean and eastern Pacific Ocean.But can be found in countries such as the united states, commonly found of the coasts of Florida and New Jersey.

They are often found around mangroves, bays, coral reefs and shallow coastal areas.

Diet

Lemon sharks mainly eat bony fish, but can also eat crustaceans and other marine animals, such as stinrays, eagel rays, mollusks like octopus, sea turtles, and sea birds.

Conservation

Lemon sharks face threats from fishing and habitat loss. Protecting important coastal habitats helps support their populations.

Interesting Fact

Lemon sharks can return to familiar nursery areas, where young sharks grow in relatively sheltered waters. But also unlike other shark species they are highly sociable and within their special bonds with others can redyce stress and improve surivival!
