Species Profile

Scientific Classification

Family: Alopiidae

Genus: Alopias

Common name: Thresher Shark

There are three recognised living species of thresher shark.

Physical Characteristics

Thresher sharks are famous for their extremely long upper tail lobes.Their tails can be nearly as long as the rest of their bodies! And they use this to their advantage in huntign their prey using their long tail like a whip to stun their prey.

And also if you ever seen one look at their adorable face! They always look like they are offended!

Habitat and Distribution

Thresher sharks live in tropical and temperate oceans around the world. But can be found in some countries such as the Philippines and Indonesia!

Depending on the species, they can be found in coastal waters and the open ocean. But to find them easily they can be found in the warm waters of the Indo-Pacific region.

Diet

97% of Thresher sharks diets are small schooling fish such as Sardines and Anchovies and also squid. They are also well known for using their long tails to stun their prey before catching it.

Behaviour

Some thresher sharks are capable of leaping out of the water. Why? Well they primarily do it for hunting this is known as breaching, allowing them to surprise and ambush fast moving prey.

They can also dive into deeper water while searching for food, reaching up to 650 meters (2,137 feet) of water depth.

Conservation

Thresher sharks face threats from commercial fishing and accidental capture in fishing equipment.

Some species are classified as threatened, so conservation and monitoring are important.

Interesting Fact

Since their unique tail is their main tool for catching prey, Their tails are also a reason of why they are sometimes called fox sharks, derived from the Greek word alṓpēx meaning fox
