LEOPARD SHARK

Shark Species Profile

Home Shark Species Habitats Research

Scientific Classification

Scientific name: Triakis semifasciata

Family: Triakidae

Average length: Around 1.2–1.5 metres

Conservation status: Least Concern

Physical Characteristics

Leopard sharks have slender bodies with distinctive dark saddle-like markings and spots across their backs and sides getting their name as Leapord sharks, their pattern helps make them easy to recognise.

Habitat and Distribution

Leopard sharks live in shallow coastal waters along the Pacific coast of North America, especially around California. They are often found over sandy or muddy seafloors and in sheltered bays and estuaries.

Diet

Leopard sharks eat small fish, crabs, shrimp, clams, worms and other bottom-dwelling animals.

Behaviour

Leopard sharks often gather in groups in shallow water. And they are also Nocturnal meaning they hunt in the night meanwhile in the daytime they often lie motionless on sandy patched between rocks and coral reefs.

Interesting Fact

Leopard sharks can sometimes be seen resting together in shallow coastal areas! They use electroreception to detect prey buried in sediment and employ rapid suction to capture it. Their generalist diet allows them to thrive in diverse habitats
